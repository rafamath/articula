# Articula

A free, open-source mobile app (iOS and Android) for people living with autoimmune and
inflammatory joint conditions, such as rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, lupus and
ankylosing spondylitis.

Log which joints hurt, how much, for how long and what they look like. Then generate a report
to show your doctor.

> **Not a medical device.** Articula does not diagnose, interpret or give medical advice. It
> records what you log and shows it back to you and your doctor.

## Principles

- **Log, don't interpret.** No scores, suggestions or "insights". Interpretation is your doctor's job.
- **Works when you're in pain.** A log takes under 30 seconds, with large tap targets and little typing.
- **Accessible from day one.** Screen readers, large text and color-independent visuals.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** No account, no server, no analytics.
- **Free forever.** No ads, no paywalls, no in-app purchases.

Early development. Not yet usable. See the milestones for progress.
